# Orientation Before Adaptation

Working files for a 150–180-page philosophical book written by Sean Cranbury and developed from the ideas and practice of Sympathetic Technology.

## Current working title

**Orientation Before Adaptation**  
*A Sympathetic Philosophy of Technology, Organizations, and Human Intention*

## Target

- 48,000–52,000 finished words
- approximately 150–180 printed pages
- first-person philosophical nonfiction
- intellectually serious, accessible, situated, and personal

## Working documents

- `prospectus.md` — purpose, argument, audience, form, and positioning
- `source-map.md` — current evidence base and missing personal material
- `voice-guide.md` — practical guide to Sean’s voice and recurring rhetorical moves
- `outline.md` — complete book architecture and chapter targets
- `AUTHORSHIP.md` — fixed boundary between Sean’s authorship and architectural/editorial support
- `process/` — documentary archive recording the making of the book

## Drafting principle

The website is source material, not a manuscript to be padded. Existing language may be retained where Sean decides it is definitive, but the book must deepen the argument, introduce lived experience, create transitions, withstand repetition, and arrive somewhere the individual essays cannot reach alone.

Sean writes the manuscript. Codex maintains the architecture and documentary record and provides editorial support within the boundaries defined in `AUTHORSHIP.md`.
